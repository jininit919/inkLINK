What InkLink is

A booking platform for tattoo artists and their clients. Artists publish their portfolio and the dates they are free; clients browse the work, pick a design and book a slot — with the deposit paid up front instead of haggled over in direct messages.

  1. Find an artist. Browse healed work and available designs, filter by style, city or price, or look them up on the map.
  2. Book a slot. Open the artist's calendar, pick a free block and pay the deposit or the full price through Stripe.
  3. Get tattooed. The money is held by Stripe and released to the artist once the session is confirmed as done — safer for both sides.

Artists can pull their existing portfolio straight from their Instagram business account instead of re-uploading it, keep a calendar, message clients and take deposits in one place.
